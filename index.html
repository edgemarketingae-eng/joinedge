This is not a beginner trading course

You may already know charts, entries, indicators and strategies, and still struggle to produce consistent results. The problem may be:

  • Overtrading or revenge trading
  • Poor risk and lot-size management
  • Strategy hopping
  • Emotional entries and exits
  • No clear trading routine
  • Repeating the same mistakes
  • Depending completely on signals

The Edge Mentorship Program is designed to identify and correct your personal trading problems.

What you will receive

Trading journey audit

A detailed review of your previous trades, strategy, risk, routine, psychology and loss patterns.

Personal problem identification

We identify whether your main issue is strategy, execution, discipline, psychology or risk management.

Strategy building

A structured trading system built around your market, available time, capital and trading behaviour.

Live-market guidance

Learn to analyse, plan, execute, manage and review trades in real market conditions.

Performance review

Your trades and decisions are reviewed regularly to find mistakes and improve your process.

Your 3-month journey

  1. Month 1

    Audit & diagnosis

    Understand what went wrong and identify the habits, decisions and systems causing losses.

  2. Month 2

    Strategy & risk rebuilding

    Build clear entry, exit, stop-loss, position-sizing and daily risk rules.

  3. Month 3

    Live execution & refinement

    Apply the system in live markets with mentor guidance, trade reviews and continuous improvement.

Who this program is for

  • Traders who already have experience

  • Are struggling with consistency
  • Need personal guidance and accountability
  • Want to rebuild their strategy and risk system
  • Are serious about improving their process

Not suitable for
